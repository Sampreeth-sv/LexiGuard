# LexiGuard Sample Data

## synthetic_contract.txt
A synthetic demonstration services agreement for testing LexiGuard.
Contains realistic but entirely fictional clauses spanning:
- Parties and Term
- Payment and late fees
- Automatic renewal
- Termination (cause and early termination penalties)
- Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
- Limitation of Liability and Indemnification
- Binding Arbitration and Class Action Waivers
- Governing Law and Non-Compete
- Data Handling and Privacy (GDPR)
- Modification rights

## synthetic_contract_v2.txt
A modified version for document comparison testing.
Key differences from v1: 
- Payment changed from $10,000 to $12,500/month
- Auto-renewal notice period changed from 30 to 60 days
- Termination penalty clause strengthened (3 months fees instead of 2)
- Added a new Data Security clause
- Removed the non-compete clause
- Liability cap changed from 3 months to 6 months

## Privacy Notice
All parties, organizations, and terms in these documents are entirely fictional.
Do not use real legal documents for testing without appropriate authorization.
